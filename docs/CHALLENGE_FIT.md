## 3. Mapping to the chosen challenge statement

**Statement:** [Population Health] Agentic AI lab for Protecting Brain Health Earlier (Future Health Digital Innovation Challenge 2026). The statement asks how technology can "protect brain health earlier by identifying meaningful changes in cognitive and functional health over time", integrate risk factors, and translate findings into "culturally appropriate, personalized prevention and timely clinical care". It notes that a one-off score can mislead (language, education, culture, sensory impairment, mood, digital familiarity) and that tests not validated for the local population produce false positives, anxiety or false reassurance.

**Our framing.** Chào Bà is not a cognitive test. It is the family's daily touchpoint on Mom's screen, and that touchpoint does two things:

1. **Prevention.** Social isolation is one of the 14 potentially modifiable risk factors for dementia in the 2024 Lancet Commission report (Livingston et al.); the WHO Commission on Social Connection (2025) links loneliness to poorer health and early death. Daily family presence is the intervention, and it is measured as its own weekly series (never fed into the light).
2. **Measurement.** The daily voice chat is a repeated, low-burden, culturally native measurement channel. Each person is compared with herself, which side-steps much of the language, education and culture bias of a one-off population-normed score.

**Agentic AI**, concretely: the Út Thương conversation agent; an orchestrator with tools and layered safety (emergency spotter before any LLM, guardrails, strict tool calls, structured post-call analysis); and the DMAIC care loop that follows each alert through action and checks whether it helped. Optional Gemini Live voice engine: first live run on 4 Nov 2026 was **no-go** (latency, skipped control tool), so the browser voice stays the default (TECHNICAL_DOCUMENTATION §7.4).
